National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education of ST Students
Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
The scheme provides financial assistance of Rs. 31000 per month for M.Phil and Rs. 35000 per month for Ph.D scholars.
Eligibility: ST candidates who have qualified UGC-NET or CSIR-NET.
Family income limit: Rs. 8.0 lakh per annum.
Last date to apply: 30 November 2026. Apply online at https://tribal.nic.in
